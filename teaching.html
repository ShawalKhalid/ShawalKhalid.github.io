Teaching Philosophy

I build courses around a sequence of explanation, implementation, and evaluation. In Python, students work directly with code and data; in machine learning, they test models and examine the consequences of design choices; and in security, they investigate concrete threats and defenses. My goal is for students to explain not only how a system works, but also when it might fail and why.
